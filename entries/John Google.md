# The Legendary Tale of John Google

## The Man, The Myth, The Search Engine

In 1998, **John Google** was a struggling shoe salesman in Palo Alto who kept forgetting where he left his car keys. One day, after spending 3 hours searching for them (they were in his pocket), he had an epiphany: 

> "What if there was a way to search for things... but on the internet?"

His wife, Susan Google, said *"John, that's the dumbest thing I've ever heard. Also, your last name is Johnson."* But John was undeterred.

## The Birth of a Vision

He changed his legal name to **John Google** that very afternoon and started the company in his garage, which he also couldn't find without asking for directions. 

The first version of Google could only search for one thing: **itself**. When you typed anything into the search bar, it just returned:
```
Did you mean: Google?
```

## The Accidental Algorithm

The breakthrough came when John's cat, **Larry Page** (yes, he named his cat Larry Page), walked across the keyboard and accidentally created the PageRank algorithm. John thought the cat was just trying to tell him it was hungry, but it turns out the cat was a genius.

## The Skeptics

Investors were skeptical. 

"Why would anyone need to search the internet?" they asked. "There's only like 100 websites." 

But John Google persisted, fueled entirely by energy drinks and the burning desire to never lose his keys again.

## The Legacy

And that's how John Google became the richest man who never actually existed.

**The end.**

---

*Disclaimer: This is completely made up. Google was actually founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin, two Stanford PhD students. No Johns or cats were involved in the actual founding.*
