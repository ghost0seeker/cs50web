# Google LLC

Google LLC is an American multinational technology company specializing in internet-related services and products.

## Key Areas of Operation:

*   **Search Engine:** The most widely used web search engine globally.
*   **Online Advertising:** Through platforms like Google Ads and AdSense.
*   **Cloud Computing:** Offering services via Google Cloud Platform.
*   **Software Development:** Including the Android operating system, Google Chrome browser, and various productivity applications (Google Workspace).
*   **Hardware:** Producing devices such as Pixel smartphones, Google Nest smart home devices, and Fitbit wearables.
*   **Artificial Intelligence:** Extensive research and development in AI, integrated across many of its products.
*   **Autonomous Vehicles:** Through its subsidiary Waymo.
*   **YouTube:** The world's largest video-sharing platform.

## History:

*   **Founded:** September 4, 1998, by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were PhD students at Stanford University.
*   **Initial Public Offering (IPO):** August 19, 2004.
*   **Restructuring:** In 2015, Google became the largest subsidiary of the newly formed holding company, Alphabet Inc.

## Significance:

Google plays a significant role in the digital landscape, impacting how information is accessed, how businesses operate online, and how individuals interact with technology.
